Water sensor, calibration & filtering → ESP32_A

Changes are sent over ESP-NOW and saved by ESP32_A. A reboot of ESP32_A is not normally required.

Mirror person detection

The mirror turns off immediately when the person moves beyond the OFF distance or the sensor loses them. Manual Mirror Light controls stay available on the dashboard.

Buzzer

Beeps per second ≈ 1000 ÷ beep period. Intensity uses PWM and is approximate on active buzzers; some modules only click instead of changing volume.

OLED display

Approximate 128×64 preview. Large digits use 1 px smooth curves, not scaled block pixels.

History storage

Retention is automatic: the oldest CSV lines are removed when the configured window is exceeded. Storage is LittleFS on ESP32_B.

Maintenance actions
